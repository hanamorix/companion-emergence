## 3. What the two fields feed, and the measured risk
**AF.7** `recall_count` feeds exactly one thing: the salience recall arm, `0.20 x min(recall_count / 10, 1)`, used by the forgetting pass. It feeds no ranker: `rank_memories` scores BM25 match 1.0, importance 0.5, hebbian 0.3 and recency 0.2, where recency is `0.5 ** (age_days / 30)` computed from `created_at` (`relevance.py:45-48`, :225-235). The semantic path ranks by reranker score. A grep of `brain/` for `recall_count` and `last_accessed` finds readers only in `forgetting/salience.py`, `initiate/resonance.py` and the display in `cli.py:630`, plus the migrator and recovery copies. [salience.py:22-33, :80-81; relevance.py:45-48, :225-235; git grep at 44af2447 | H]
**AF.8** `last_accessed_at` feeds: the salience freshness arm, weight 0.10, `1 - lived_hours_since(anchor) / (2160 x (1 + 52 x importance/10))` with the anchor `last_accessed_at or created_at`; the peak linger inside the emotion arm (weight 0.30 on the larger of live emotion and the peak term, anchor the same, horizon 4320 lived hours); the recall-resonance staleness gate (a memory must be at least 7 days stale, `gate_thresholds.json:11`, `resonance.py:208, :313-316`); the CLI display; and, once A3 lands, the rebuild window (`max(created_at, last_accessed_at)` within 30 days, A3 E7.9, and "accessed-only" days count as active days, E7.14). The fade line is `salience < 0.25 / (1 + narrative_weight + 2.0 x importance/10)` and the lost line is `< 0.10` for 2 passes. [salience.py:125-155, :34, :41, :49; policy.py:22-24, :35, :73-76; resonance.py:208, :313-316; A3 E7.9, E7.14 | H]
**AF.9** What this part changes is therefore small. The +1.0 does not change (AF.5). The reappraisal enqueue does not change. The things that do change for a memory shown in full are: (a) its `last_accessed_at` is now, so the freshness arm (<= 0.10 of salience) and the peak linger restart, and the recall-resonance staleness clock restarts (a memory she was just shown is not "stale" for 7 days: the meaning the owner gave "last-accessed"); (b) it reaches the engine once per open, where the engine's repeat rule halves the weight within 24 hours (A3 E1.10, E2.4); (c) a rebuild sees it as accessed at that time. (a) is a change in what the Kindled experiences (which memories fade). Today (a) is masked for the top 50 hebbian-active memories, because the resonance tick sets `last_accessed_at` for each of them every 15 minutes (AF.6); AF.17a stops that, so after this part a memory's `last_accessed_at` moves when it is shown in full (and through the reads of bycatch 4 to 6 until they are fixed), and the freshness restart is no longer overwritten by the tick.
